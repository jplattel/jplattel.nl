I've been reading on [Xteink](https://www.xteink.com/) devices in the past year (and succesfully destroyed 2 of them, an X4 and an X3... next up to order was the X4 Pro (with backlight!). You might wonder why I keep on order stuff after I break them.. but I'm kind of addicted to the pocket format and being able to read quickly instead of using my mobile phone.

My reading habits have increased over the past year since getting a device like this. However the [X4 Pro](https://www.xteink.com/products/xteink-x4-pro-pocket-ereader) is too large to fit next to my phone (with magsafe). I also was due for a new wallet and thus figured it was time to tinker around!

I've made a 3D model that fits cards from my wallet as well as the X4 Pro (a combination I haven't seen yet!). Here are a couple of photos:

![](https://files.jplattel.nl/2026/10/6v4BsA.JPG)

![](https://files.jplattel.nl/2026/10/AAwJSL.png)

And the model is [up on Makerworld](https://makerworld.com/en/models/3433675-xteink-x4-pro-card-wallet#profileId-3915001) for download! You'll need 2 threaded inserts and a couple of M3 bolts, but other then that it'll work! (I can recommend printing it with a PETG/PLA mix for interface layers for support).
